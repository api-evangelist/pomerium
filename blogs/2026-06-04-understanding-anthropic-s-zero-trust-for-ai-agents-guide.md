Most writing about AI agent security stops at the scary part: agents can be prompt-injected, they hold credentials, they act near production. True, and not very useful. However, the implementation half of Anthropic's Zero Trust for AI Agents guide is more interesting because it stops describing the problem and starts grading the solutions.
