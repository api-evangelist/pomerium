Shadow AI refers to the use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity) without approval or oversight from security or compliance teams. These tools often enter organizations through bottom-up adoption. Employees turn to them to improve productivity, automate tasks, or write code faster.
