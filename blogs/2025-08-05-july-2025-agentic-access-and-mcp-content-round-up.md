July 2025 Agentic Access and MCP Content Round‑Up: Vulnerabilities, Governance & Growth A number of pivotal developments marked the agentic AI and Model Context Protocol (MCP) world this past July. Two critical remote‑code‑execution flaws, one in the widely used mcp‑remote tool and another in Anthropic’s MCP Inspector , highlighted the growing security risks of AI‑agent tooling. Meanwhile, vendors released new MCP servers and training programs, and the open‑source project announced a formal governance model to sustain its rapid growth.
