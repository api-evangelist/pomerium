TL;DR — The OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model (LLM) applications highlights prompt injection, insecure output handling, and data exposure as critical risks. This guide walks through the Top 10 and shows where Zero Trust access controls, starting free with Pomerium Zero , are most effective: LLM01 Prompt Injection and LLM02 Sensitive Information Disclosure. Why the OWASP Top 10 Matters OWASP publishes the most recognized list of software security risks.
